Two B’s, one shared 2

Every mark is built the same way: two bold geometric B’s are overlapped into a single form, and the channel they leave between them is cut as the number 2. Nothing is drawn twice — the 2 only exists as the letters’ negative space.

01  Spine The most compact and the most legible small — but it reads as a 2 first and resolves into two B’s second.
02  Slab Holds together at 16 px better than anything else here, which makes it the natural app icon and favicon.
03  Interlock The most letter-like of the bold marks; the stagger costs it some of the Spine’s calm.
04  Rotor Reads as an exchange between two people, which suits passing a list around; busiest of the five up close.
05  Monoline By far the clearest as two B’s, and the weakest as a 2 — the lightest, most editorial register of the set.
