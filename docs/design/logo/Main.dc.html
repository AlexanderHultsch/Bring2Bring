Two B’s, one shared 2

Every mark is built the same way: two geometric capital B’s are overlapped into a single form, and the channel they leave between them is cut as the number 2. Nothing is drawn twice — the 2 only exists as the letters’ negative space.

01Spine The calmest and most compact of the five — but it reads as a 2 first, and resolves into two B’s only on a second look.
02Slab Holds together at 16 px better than anything else here, which makes it the natural app icon and favicon.
03Interlock The most letter-like of the bold marks; the stagger costs it some of the Spine’s calm.
04Rotor Reads as an exchange between two people, which suits passing a list around; busiest of the five up close.
05Monoline By far the clearest as two B’s, and the weakest as a 2 — the lightest, most editorial register of the set.
